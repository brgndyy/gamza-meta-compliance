Permissions used in this flow

Reviewer-visible flow

  1. Press Start live test and post the exact keyword on the configured Reel.
  2. The app receives the comment webhook and sends one public reply and one Instagram Private Reply.
  3. Reply with the same keyword in Instagram Direct and press the follow-check button before following.
  4. Open the business profile, follow it in Instagram, return to the DM, and check again.
  5. The app verifies the relationship and sends the requested information once.
